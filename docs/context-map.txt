SEATWAVE CONTEXT MAP (Module 32)

CONTEXTS: what each one decides, and its language
  catalog       what is on sale, where and at what price. "event" = a show; "seat" = a place on a
                plan (section, number); writes rarely, read 20,000 times a second at the peak.
  inventory     who may claim which seat, and for how long. "seat" = a claimable unit of one show,
                with a state; "hold" = a 300-second claim; the hottest writes (13,333 a second).
  booking       turning a hold into a paid purchase. "booking" = a purchase and its saga row;
                orders the steps of the confirm and publishes BookingConfirmed.
  payment       taking and returning money once per provider key. "charge", "capture", "refund";
                the only context that talks to the payment provider.
  notification  what the fan is told, and when. "event" = a domain event it reacts to; "booking"
                = a reason to send a message.

RELATIONSHIPS: upstream -> downstream, type, and what crosses
  catalog -> inventory       open host service + conformist: inventory asks Catalog.offerFor
                             whether a seat exists and keeps the catalog's seat identity
                             (event_id, seat_label) exactly
  catalog -> booking         customer-supplier: booking reads the offer (price, show start)
                             through Catalog.offerFor; booking's needs shape that interface
  inventory -> booking       customer-supplier: booking calls lockForPayment, confirm, release
  payment -> booking         customer-supplier: booking calls charge with payment's own Money;
                             booking translates the catalog's price into it
  payment provider -> payment  anticorruption layer: payment turns the provider's codes and
                             references into Captured or Declined; no provider type leaves it
  booking -> notification    published language + conformist: notification reacts to
                             BookingConfirmed and keeps its own copy of what it needs
  identity (Module 11 tokens) -> every context  open host service: the token's subject is the
                             fan; SeatWave conforms to it
  platform <-> every context shared kernel: Database (local transactions) and DomainEvents
                             (in-process publication); no business rule; changed only with the
                             agreement of every module's owner

RULES
  1. No module reads or writes another module's tables or uses its internal classes.
  2. A module's tables are reached only through that module's interface or its events.
  3. One local transaction may span modules only through their interfaces, inside one
     deployable and one database; the provider call never runs inside a transaction.
  4. No foreign key crosses a schema.
  5. A module becomes a service only when the extraction rule of Section 6 says so.
